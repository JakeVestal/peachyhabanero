TREASURY CUBES

These two cubes are the Treasury’s problem, not the Fed’s. The stock, the till, the book coupon, and whether a hike is fiscally unthinkable are fiscal objects. The Fed’s mandate is prices, employment, and moderate long-term rates — not the best financing position of the United States.

The Sustainability Cube (left) defines a Danger Zone within which the US debt stock is at high risk of entering a self-reinforcing feedback loop of runaway debt and interest, as well as a Death Zone in which such a feedback loop is all but certain.

The Fiscal Dominance Cube (right) is still a Treasury question: the region in which a rate hike would be unthinkable on fiscal arithmetic even if inflation printed hot and the Fed’s inflation mandate would otherwise demand one. That is the contest — Treasury cannot support the hike; the mandate may still require it.

Distance to the Danger Zone / Death Zone over time

Tracks score-space distance to the nearest point on the Danger (s≥1) or Death Zone (s≥2) octant. This distance is positive if a set of quarterly prints falls outside or on a face, and negative if inside. Note that this metric is a calculated distance across all three score-space dimensions, and is not a raw percent or basis point measure.

Point color on the sustainability path is interest-burden stress: a 0–1 blend of the three score-space coordinates (20% debt/GDP, 35% interest share, 25% refi gap) plus a 20% sleeve for interest / GDP (`A091 / GDP`), piecewise through 3.0% (warn) and 4.5% (death). That sleeve is not a cube axis. It is only the color so a high till-share quarter that is still a small slice of GDP does not look the same as one that is already a program.

Description of Each Axis and its Tripwire Levels

1 · Interest / Till

Share of this quarter’s incoming cash (the “till”) already spoken for by interest on the existing book, and not available to fund the government's operations.

100 × A091RC1Q027SBEA / FGRECPT   (tax toggle: / W006RC1Q027SBEA)

Interest and the till are both NIPA SAAR so the ratio stays inside one vintage. Intrest is fetched from FRED item A091RC1Q027SBEA By default, the till is set to current receipts (FGRECPT): taxes, social-insurance contributions, income on assets, and the rest of the current-receipts envelope. That is the cash that can service the book. The buttons above the cubes switch the denominator to tax receipts only (W006RC1Q027SBEA): same theory, smaller till.

The Danger Zone is 20% of receipts. Federal receipts run near 17% of GDP; 20% × 0.17 ≈ 3.4% of GDP of interest — the same order as OMB function 050 (national defense). 0.03 / 0.17 ≈ 18, rounded to 20.

The Death Zone is 30%. Same map: 30% × 0.17 ≈ 5% of GDP of interest. That is past the postwar US sample and well past “the same size as defense.” It is a labeled step on that identity, not a fitted cliff.

2 · Refi Gap

The Refi Gap is the difference between the marginal rate the book would clear at if it were marked to the current Treasury curve by remaining maturity, and the coupon that the marketable stock already carries.

marginal − stock coupon

The Treasury issues new debt to pay what is already out. If the gap is positive, new issue costs more than the stock and the book’s carrying rate has to rise as it rolls. If the gap is negative, rolls cheapen the book.

Stock coupon is not constructed. It is the Treasury Fiscal Data print Average Interest Rates on U.S. Treasury Securities, row Total Marketable (v2/accounting/od/avg_interest_rates). That is the carrying rate on marketable debt only.

Marginal is constructed. Each month we take every marketable CUSIP on MSPD Table 3, remaining life = maturity − record date, and collapse principal into buckets. Those weights times the CMT stand-in for that bucket:

0–1y × TB3MS + 1–3y × DGS2 + 3–7y × DGS5 + 7–10y × DGS10 + 10y+ × DGS30 + FRN × FEDFUNDS

TIPS are not in that sum. The TIPS yield we would use (DFII10) is real; the coupon we subtract is nominal. Mixing them would move the gap for a unit reason. TIPS stay in the Table 3 weights so the share is visible; the other buckets are renormalized. FRNs stay in the sum (FEDFUNDS is already nominal).

3 · Debt held by the public / GDP

Reported directly by FRED, Debt Held by the Public as a percentage of GDP ( FYGFGDQ188S) provides the of existing debt stock the that the till and the coupon have to carry; in other words, it reflects the overall debt burden that taxpayers and the government must manage and pay interest on. This is distinct from gross federal debt ( GFDEGDQ188S), which includes intra-governmental IOUs that the government writes to itself and do not roll into the market.

The Danger Zone for Debt held by the public/GDP is set to100% following the round figure that the Congressional Budget Office (CBO) and the IMF Fiscal Monitor have identified as a warning level for fiscal risk. The Death Zone was set to 140%, because at that point the US would reach Italy/Greece-crisis levels, but without the advantage of Japan’s captive domestic bid for refinancing.

4 · y(funds − book coupon)

Same metric as 2 · Refi gap except the left-hand rate is FEDFUNDS instead of m: funds − Fiscal Data Total Marketable coupon. This plot is the cube coordinate, not the raw gap:

F1 = −(x₁ − 0) / σ

, σ from 2000-01-01. Sign is flipped so F1 > 0 means funds are already at or under the book — a hike is then a fiscal tightening of the stock.

5 · y(Interest / Receipts)

Same series as axis 1 with till=receipts, except transformed as a sigma-scaled distance from the threshold. 20% is subtracted from each measurement of Interest/Revenue and divided by the standard deviation from 2000-01-01.

6 primary / GDP

Are operations still open. Magenta wire at 0: still borrowing after interest.

100 × (FGEXPND − A091RC1Q027SBEA − FGRECPT) / GDP

All four of the named series are NIPA / BEA SAAR, same vintage as axis 1. Uses primary deficit, not the headline unified deficit: interest is stripped so this axis asks “are we borrowing for operations,” not “is the till already paying the coupon” (that is axes 1 and 5). If positive, we're borrowing for operations; otherwise if negative. Together with axis 3 completes the stock identity Δ(debt/GDP) ≈ (r − g)×stock + primary.
