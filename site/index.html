FED CUBES

The Sustainibility Cube (left) defines a Danger Zone within which the US debt stock is at high risk of entering a self-reinforcing feedback loop of runaway debt and interest, as well as a Death Zone in which such a feedback loop is all but certain.

The Fiscal Dominance Cube (right) plots the answer to a similar question by defining a region within which the Fed must abandon its inflation-fighting mandate in order to service existing debt. Within the cube, a rate hike would be unthinkable even if inflation printed hot and the Fed would otherwise like to raise rates.

Use these buttons to toggle between displaying "Interest/Tax" and "Interest/Receipts" on the Sustainability Cube and the 1-D distance plot below. "Tax" means total collected tax revenue (W006RC1Q027SBEA in the FRED database). "Receipts" means total govenmental reciepts (FGRECPT), which includes taxes plus Social Security and Medicare payroll taxes, fines, income on assets, and all other sources of government income.

Distance to the Danger Zone / Death Zone over time

Tracks score-space distance to the nearest point on the Danger (s≥1) or Death Zone (s≥2) octant. This distance is positive if a set of quarterly prints falls outside or on a face, and negative if inside. Note that this metric is a calculated distance across all three score-space dimensions, and is not a raw percent or basis point measure.
