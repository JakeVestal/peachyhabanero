TREASURY CUBES / METHODOLOGY

Two cubes, two sentences, six axes. The left cube asks whether the stock is already in a self-reinforcing region. The right cube asks whether a hike would be unthinkable on fiscal arithmetic even if inflation printed hot. Axes 1 and 4 are the same series in two embeddings. Axes 2 and 5 share a book coupon and then use different rates. Axes 3 and 6 are a stock and a flow linked by an identity, not two spellings of one number.

00 / what this is not

The wires are conventions, not estimated thresholds. They are not Reinhart–Rogoff 90, not a Blanchard r−g test, and not a CBO long-run projection. A quarter inside a box is “these three doors are on,” not “default next Tuesday.” Falsify an axis by showing the named series does not measure the sentence on that axis. Do not falsify it by preferring a different sentence.

01 / vintage rules that apply to every axis

Quarterly last observation in the quarter. NIPA interest, receipts, outlays, and GDP stay in NIPA. Market rates stay FRED constant-maturity / funds. The book coupon stays Treasury Fiscal Data. We do not mix a unified-budget FY print with a NIPA SAAR denominator on the same axis. Debt held by the public, never gross debt. The identity that links the stock cube to the hole is

Δ(debt/GDP) ≈ (r − g) × stock + primary

r here is the effective coupon on the book, g is nominal GDP growth. That identity is why axes 3 and 6 are both on the site and why they are not substitutes. Metric 05 on the data page reports r−g. It is not a cube axis.

02 / the book coupon (shared by axes 2 and 5)

One number is “the stock.” Both roll axes subtract it.

stock coupon · Treasury average interest rate, total marketable

Source: Treasury Fiscal Data, Average Interest Rates on U.S. Treasury Securities — security class Total Marketable.

Why this and not NIPA interest / debt. Fiscal Data’s published average rate is the coupon the marketable book actually carries. NIPA A091RC1Q027SBEA over debt held by the public is an effective rate: it folds in premiums and discounts, nonmarketable issues, and intra-governmental accounts. Fine as a robustness line on the data page. Wrong as the book that new issuance is compared to.

03 / axis 1 — interest share of the till (sustainability Y)

Sentence: how much of this quarter’s incoming cash is already spoken for by interest. Level, not a z-score. Default denominator is current receipts. The tax toggle is a tighter till, not a different theory.

interest · A091RC1Q027SBEA

Federal government interest payments, NIPA, SAAR $bn. FRED A091RC1Q027SBEA.

receipts · FGRECPT

Federal government current receipts, NIPA, SAAR $bn. Taxes plus contributions for social insurance, income on assets, and the rest of the current-receipts envelope. FRED FGRECPT.

tax receipts · W006RC1Q027SBEA

Federal government current tax receipts, NIPA, SAAR $bn. FRED W006RC1Q027SBEA.

int/rec = 100 × A091RC1Q027SBEA / FGRECPT
int/tax = 100 × A091RC1Q027SBEA / W006RC1Q027SBEA

Warn / death on this axis are 20 / 30 percent of receipts, or 25 / 40 percent of tax. Those are labeled conventions. Unified-budget outlays/receipts are fiscal-year and are not used here: they would break the quarterly path and the GDP vintage on the other two left-hand axes.

04 / axis 4 — F2, same share past the wire

Same raw series as axis 1. The right cube does not invent a second interest-burden concept. It embeds the level in the units of the failure octant: y > 0 means that door is on.

F2_rec = (int/rec − 20) / σ(int/rec)
F2_tax = (int/tax − 25) / σ(int/tax)

σ is the sample standard deviation of the quarterly complete-case series. The receipts / tax toggle moves axis 1 and F2 together. Rejected: putting ACM term premium or NFCI on this axis. Those are amplifiers on the data page. They do not define the till.

05 / axis 2 — refi gap (sustainability Z)

Sentence: is the market already refinancing the book above the coupon the book carries. This is the strip the chair does not have to touch. The marginal rate is the issuance mix that quarter, not a fixed 25/50/25 prior.

front · TB3MS

3-month Treasury bill, secondary market, monthly average, percent. FRED TB3MS. Stands in for bills.

belly · DGS2

2-year Treasury constant maturity, percent. FRED DGS2.

back · DGS10

10-year Treasury constant maturity, percent. FRED DGS10.

mix · MSPD bills share of marketable, else auction volumes

Preferred weight on the front is MSPD_BILLS_SHARE_MARKETABLE from the Monthly Statement of the Public Debt (bills outstanding held by the public / marketable held by the public). Treasury Fiscal Data, MSPD. When auction detail is complete for the quarter, issuance dollars replace the stock share: bills issued × TB3MS, notes issued in the 2-year neighborhood × DGS2, longer coupons × DGS10, weights sum to 1.

Why not 0.25 / 0.50 / 0.25. That blend is a prior. The book’s actual front-end share is observed. A bills-heavy quarter should move the marginal rate with TB3MS, not with the 10-year.

marginal = w_bills × TB3MS + w_2y × DGS2 + w_10y × DGS10
refi gap = marginal − stock coupon

Warn / death on this axis are +0.50 / +1.00 percentage points. Positive means new issuance already costs more than the book. Rejected as the axis: ACM 10-year term premium (THREEFYTP10). That is “duration is charging rent,” metric 04 on the data page, a different sentence.

06 / axis 5 — F1, funds minus the same book

Sentence: if the chair hiked, would the policy rate itself be the fiscal rate on the book. Same stock coupon as axis 2. Different comparison rate. Sargent–Wallace / fiscal theory is about the policy instrument, not the 2s10s curve.

policy · FEDFUNDS

Effective federal funds rate, percent. FRED FEDFUNDS.

funds − stock = FEDFUNDS − stock coupon
F1 = (funds − stock − 0) / σ(funds − stock)

Direction on the wire is at-or-below zero after the usual sign flip in standardization: F1 > 0 means funds are already at or under the book coupon, so a hike is a fiscal tightening of the stock. Do not replace F1 with the refi gap. The market can lift the book while the chair sits; that is why axis 2 exists.

07 / axis 3 — debt held by the public / GDP (sustainability X)

Sentence: how large is the stock that interest and refinancing act on. This is the standard sustainability stock, not gross debt.

stock ratio · FYGFGDQ188S

Federal debt held by the public as a percent of GDP, quarterly. FRED FYGFGDQ188S.

Why not GFDEBTN / GDP. Gross debt includes intra-governmental holdings. Those are not the stock the market refinances and not the stock whose interest hits the till. Debt held by the public (FYGFDPUN) over GDP is the object in Blanchard, in CBO long-run, and in the Fiscal Service table the series is built from.

Warn / death on this axis are 100 / 140 percent of GDP. Labeled stress bands, not an estimated threshold and not Reinhart 90. A robustness print of the ratio from FYGFDPUN / GDP in $bn belongs on the data page; the cube uses the published FRED ratio so the vintage matches everyone else’s chart.

08 / axis 6 — F3, primary deficit / GDP

Sentence: are operations still financed with new borrowing. A flow. High debt/GDP with a primary surplus is a different country from a wide hole under a smaller stock. F3 is not a z-score of axis 3.

outlays · FGEXPND

Federal government current expenditures, NIPA, SAAR $bn. FRED FGEXPND.

GDP · GDP

Gross domestic product, NIPA, SAAR $bn. FRED GDP.

primary $bn = (FGEXPND − A091RC1Q027SBEA) − FGRECPT
primary % GDP = 100 × primary $bn / GDP
F3 = (primary % GDP − 0) / σ(primary % GDP)

Positive primary is a deficit. The wire is zero: still borrowing for operations. NIPA, not the unified budget, so the flow shares a vintage with FGRECPT, A091RC1Q027SBEA, and GDP. Rejected as the cube axis: cyclically adjusted primary balance. GDPPOT and the unemployment gap (UNRATE − NROU) stay on the data page as “is this a cycle” checks. Putting a filter on the cube would make the fight about the filter.

10 / what would change an axis

  1. A documented Fiscal Data break in the Total Marketable average rate — then the NIPA effective coupon becomes the book, on both roll axes at once.
  2. A quarter where auction volumes exist and contradict MSPD bills share by enough to move the warn face — then auctions take priority, which is already the rule.
  3. A NIPA vs unified-budget reconciliation that made FGRECPT the wrong till for interest — then we would owe a second burden series, not a silent swap.
  4. An estimated, out-of-sample threshold for debt/GDP or the interest share that beat the labeled bands on a pre-registered rule — then the band changes. Until then the band stays a label.
