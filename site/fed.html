FED VIEW

The Fed’s mandate is stable prices, maximum employment, and moderate long-term rates. This page is that object — not the Treasury cubes. Three characteristic gaps, plus what the FOMC actually did to the funds target.

Markers are month-end changes in the upper target (DFEDTARU): magenta triangle up = hike, lime triangle down = cut. A hold is the default: no marker. Pre-range years use FEDFUNDS only when DFEDTARU is missing. Holds are the path, not extra markers.

Mandate space

Each month is a point: inflation gap (X), employment gap (Y), ACM 10y term premium (Z). Cyan path is the hold default. Magenta = hike, lime = cut.

What they did — funds and the target

Effective funds and the published target range. Supporting plot, not the mandate object.

1 · Inflation gap · PCE yoy − 2%

Headline personal consumption expenditures price index, twelve-month percent change, minus the FOMC’s 2% longer-run goal.

100 × (PCEPIt / PCEPIt−12 − 1) − 2

Level is FRED PCEPI, monthly. The 2% target is applied to the whole sample, including pre-2012, with that vintage labeled — the FOMC did not adopt an explicit 2% goal until January 2012. Positive = inflation above target (mandate pressure to hike). Core PCE (PCEPILFE) stays in the raw pull; it is not this axis.

2 · Employment gap · NROU − UNRATE

Slack versus the FOMC’s published natural rate. Positive = unemployment below NROU (mandate looking met on jobs). Negative = slack.

NROU − UNRATE

UNRATE is monthly civilian unemployment. NROU is CBO’s natural rate, quarterly; we hold the last quarterly print across months in that quarter. This is the Board’s own longer-run unemployment concept, not a u* we estimated.

3 · Long rates · ACM 10y term premium

What duration charges beyond the expected path of short rates and inflation. The long-rate mandate is “moderate,” not a 2% target; this series is the residual the Board actually debates.

THREEFYTP10

NY Fed ACM three-factor 10-year term premium, FRED THREEFYTP10. Starts 1990. Not DGS10 (path + inflation + premium) and not DFII10 (real yield). Those stay on scratch.
