01 // Who is who

Apex Franchise Capital (stand-in for an originator like Exponent) makes fixed-rate loans and business cards to multi-unit franchisees. They want to grow the book faster than equity allows.

Jovian provides a $20mm senior secured warehouse. Jovian does not underwrite each franchisee. Jovian underwrites the pool and the originator’s ability to make and service it.

The student is the Jovian analyst and the engineer who has to make the borrowing base run.

02 // The vehicle

Receivables sit in a bankruptcy-remote SPV. Collections hit a lockbox. The warehouse advances against a borrowing base. Later takeout can be ABS or a larger private book. The warehouse is the on-ramp.

03 // The tape (ugly on purpose)

Loan ID, origination date, product (card vs term), franchise brand, units, outstanding, days past due, state, remaining term, charge-off flag, unearned discount, last payment.

If Jovian can share a header layout or scrambled balances, we use that instead of a synthetic tape.

04 // What they compute

FIRST PASS — replace with whatever eligibility and reserves Jovian would actually run.

  1. Eligibility. Drop: >30 DPD, charge-off, concentration over 15% in one brand, remaining term < 3 months, related-party, incomplete fields.
  2. Borrowing base.
    BB = Σ eligible outstanding × advance rate − dilution reserve − loss reserve
    e.g. term 80%, card 70%; extra 5% reserve if the 30+ DPD bucket > 4%.
  3. Availability. min(commitment, BB) − already drawn.
  4. Shock. 3% extra dilution (franchisee credits); one brand is 22% of the pool. How much does availability fall? Do they breach?
  5. Plot. BB vs drawn vs commitment over 12 monthly tapes.
05 // The hole is the product

Whenever drawn > borrowing base, or the base collapses while drawn stays put, they are over-advanced. Cash has to come back, collateral has to get eligible again, or Jovian has a problem.

A human finds that hole by fighting a CSV. A system finds it every morning: eligibility flags, a dilution spike, one brand blew the cap, availability just dropped $1.4mm. The dashboard that shows the hole before the borrowing-base certificate is a lie is a product students can build.

Teaching analog: corporate direct lending goes red when a covenant test fails. ABF goes red when the base fails against the draw.

06 // Defense question

You are not underwriting the franchisee’s EBITDA. You are underwriting (a) the originator’s ability to make and service the loans and (b) the pool’s liquidation math if they blow up. Where in the structure does Jovian get paid if Apex fails?
