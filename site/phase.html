Two things move. The debt ratio, and the nominal rate accruing on the debt already outstanding. Income growth and the primary deficit are taken from the quarter you select and held still across the grid. Giving them their own laws is later.

ḋ = (r − γ) / 100 · d + p

ṙ = α (d − dcrit) − σ r

symbolmeaning
d Debt held by the public / GDP, percent. FYGFGDQ188S, or the Treasury print when FRED has not posted the quarter.
r Nominal rate accruing on that debt, percent. The average coupon on marketable Treasury debt. Not the rate on a new bond.
γ Nominal GDP growth over the previous four quarters, percent. This quarter’s, held still on the grid.
p Primary deficit / GDP, percent. Spending other than interest, minus receipts. This quarter’s, held still on the grid. A surplus is negative.
α Points of yield per year added for each point of debt ratio above the threshold. A knob.
dcrit Debt ratio at which the rate is not being pushed up. A knob.
σ How hard the rate is pulled back down, per year. A knob.

The gold curve is where the debt ratio is not changing. The magenta line is where the rate is not changing. Each arrow is the step those two equations give in one year. The shafts share one scale, so a longer one is a larger step. The hover is the step itself, in percentage points per year.
