STATUS MONITOR

Simple live aggregation of official status endpoints for major cloud, CDN, and service providers. Better signal than a search when something feels off. Client-side only; refreshes every 60s.

Last updated: —

Notes

Live-polled (CORS-friendly JSON): Cloudflare, Google Cloud, Optimizely. AWS, Azure, Microsoft 365, Apple, and CISA show a cyan ring indicator because their public status feeds are either missing, redirected, encoded in a non-browser-friendly way, or blocked by CORS from a static page. Click through for the official source.

This is not a substitute for official communications or your own monitoring. If a major coordinated event is underway, expect clearer signals on CISA and the primary status pages.
