01 // Module Overview

Students design the decision and failure layer for a vertical that is moving from ACH to FedNow and RTP. The platform must choose a rail under real constraints (speed, cost, return risk, liquidity, fraud window) and stay consistent when an instant leg fails after a card or ACH leg has already moved.

The emphasis is on the operational problems that appear once volume is real: liquidity under 24/7 credit-push, fraud scoring inside a sub-50ms window, non-value messages, and the state machine when an RTP or FedNow payment is rejected or returned. Students produce reviewable artifacts rather than open-ended research.

02 // Core Deliverables
  • Rail decision framework — for a chosen vertical (gig payouts, real-estate escrow, or B2B invoice), when does FedNow or RTP beat ACH once returns, ops load, and liquidity cost are included?
  • Hybrid failure state machine — what happens when an RTP leg is rejected after a card authorization or ACH credit has already posted. Ledger consistency under asynchronous settlement.
  • Fraud and non-value message surface — which checks can run inside the instant window, which must be deferred, and how Request for Payment / status messages change the flow.

04 // Why This Is Useful for an Industry Partner
  • Structured external look at vertical-specific rail choice and hybrid failure modes without consuming significant internal bandwidth.
  • Early visibility into Duke FinTech talent working on production-shaped instant payment problems.
  • Fresh perspective on the operational cost of moving a vertical from ACH to FedNow/RTP once returns and fraud are real.
  • Concrete, reviewable artifacts (decision tables, state machines, short memos) that product or engineering leads can read in 15–20 minutes.
