INSTRUCTOR NOTES

Checklist for running a session on the Overnight Clearing Desk. Not for students.

Session checklist

  1. Open the lobby (overnight-clearing.html) and click Host a New Game. You will be taken to the instructor view (keep that tab; the URL contains a private token).
  2. Confirm the status bar shows the session is active. Add banks (one per student or team) or let students join themselves with the session ID. Each bank gets a private link.
  3. Share the private student links (copy from the panel or open them in new tabs).
  4. Optional: apply a shock, set the corridor (IORB / SRF), or set the timer minutes.
  5. Press Start Trading (or Start on the timer). Students can now post bids and offers.
  6. Watch the order book and tape. Crossed orders fill; the book updates live.
  7. Close the market manually or let the timer reach zero. Both paths close the same way.
  8. Advance day to accrue overnight interest, reset floating-rate assets, and open the next session.
  9. End session when finished (clears the shared state).

If students ask why it matters

The overnight repo market is the plumbing of the financial system. Banks and dealers fund inventories and manage cash overnight against Treasury collateral. SOFR is the volume-weighted median of those trades. The Fed does not set the 10-year yield, but it influences the short end through the corridor (IORB as a floor, the Standing Repo Facility as a ceiling). When that plumbing tightens or loosens, the effect shows up quickly in short-term rates and the broader cost of credit. Point them at the background note for the short version.
