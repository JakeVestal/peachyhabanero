What each number is

  1. F1 / funds − stock coupon — FEDFUNDS minus Fiscal Data Total Marketable coupon. Raw x1 = funds − coupon. Wire at 0, direction at_or_below. y1 = F1 = −(x1 − 0) / σ(x1). F1 > 0 means funds are at or under the book (a hike tightens the fiscal rate).
  2. F2 / interest ÷ receipts — NIPA federal interest over current receipts. Unthinkable at or above 20%.
  3. F3 / primary deficit ÷ GDP — (outlays − interest − receipts) / GDP. Unthinkable at or above 0.
  4. fail — AND of those three wires after standardizing through the threshold file.

y = (x − c) / σ, flipped if direction is at_or_below. s = 1 when y > 0.
