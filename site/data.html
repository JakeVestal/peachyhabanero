What each number is

  1. F1 / funds − stock coupon — effective funds minus NIPA interest ÷ debt stock. Unthinkable at or below 0: hiking is the fiscal rate.
  2. F2 / interest ÷ receipts — NIPA federal interest over current receipts. Unthinkable at or above 20%.
  3. F3 / primary deficit ÷ GDP — (outlays − interest − receipts) / GDP. Unthinkable at or above 0.
  4. Amplifiers 4–6 — term premium, r − g, NFCI. Color only. They do not define fail.
  5. fail — AND of the three fiscal wires after standardizing through the threshold file.
