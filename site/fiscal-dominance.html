FISCAL DOMINANCE CUBE

What would it take for the Fed relax its indepenent stance in pursuit of its triple mandate and begin to consider the Treasury's needs in its decisionmaking?

The boundaries on the Fiscal Dominance Cube were chosen based on one possible answer to that question. It reckons that if:

  1. the funds rate is at or under the Treasury's book rate,
  2. and interest paid to the public is over one fifth of general-fund receipts,
  3. and the primary deficit / GDP is positive

...all at the same time, then the Fed might begin, either through voluntary cooperation or Congressional action (similar to the WWII-era legislation passed under comparable conditions), to relax its independence as a money supply manager only and consider Treasury budgetary needs in its policy decisions.

01 / Distance to the Fiscal Dominance Zone over time

Tracks σ-space distance to the nearest point on the Fiscal Dominance octant (F1 ≥ 0, F2 ≥ 0, F3 ≥ 0). This distance is positive if a quarter falls outside or on a face, and negative if inside. Note that this metric is a calculated distance across all three score-space dimensions, and is not a raw percent or basis point measure.

Zero is the magenta box. Same units as the cube coordinates, not the underlying percents. The labeled 2018-Q1 point is a record, not a baseline: the deepest into the cube that the Fed has hiked in this sample.

02 / Distance and FOMC Δ

Same signed σ-distance as in 01 (negative = inside, 0 = the magenta face) on x; quarterly net FOMC Δ on y. Distance is the independent variable: did being inside the box change the hike / hold / cut. Missing FOMC prints are omitted, not plotted as a hold. Magenta ring = inside the box.
