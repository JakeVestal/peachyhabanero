FISCAL DOMINANCE CUBE

The Fiscal Dominance Cube asks a different sentence: if funds are already at or under the book coupon, interest already takes more than a fifth of the till, and the primary is still in deficit at the same time, a hike is fiscally unthinkable even if inflation printed hot.

one magenta box · three conditions at once

If the funds rate is at or under the Treasury book rate, interest is over one fifth of revenue, and primary deficit / GDP is positive all at the same time, the Fed might begin — by cooperation or by statute, as in the WWII-era yield cap — to weigh Treasury financing next to its inflation mandate.

That is an assumption about process, not a default call. Falsify it by showing those three prints can sit together without pressure on independence.
