FISCAL DOMINANCE CUBE

What would it take for the Fed relax its indepenent stance in pursuit of its triple mandate and begin to consider the Treasury's needs in its decisionmaking?

The boundaries on the Fiscal Dominance Cube were chosen based on one possible answer to that question. It reckons that if:

  1. the funds rate is at or under the Treasury's book rate,
  2. and interest payments make up over one fifth of Treasury revenue,
  3. and the primary deficit / GDP is positive

...all at the same time, then the Fed might begin, either through voluntary cooperation or Congressional action (similar to the WWII-era legislation passed under comparable conditions), to relax its independence as a money supply manager only and consider Treasury budgetary needs in its policy decisions.
